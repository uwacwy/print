How this works

  1. Scan the QR code

    Point your phone’s camera at the code posted next to the printer and tap the link that appears.

  2. Choose your documents

    Add PDFs or photos, give the job a name you’ll recognize, and set the number of copies.

  3. Collect your pages

    Tap Print and your job goes straight to that printer. Pick it up from the tray in a moment.
