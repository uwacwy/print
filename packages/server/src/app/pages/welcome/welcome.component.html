Print from any device

Print to any of our available printers without an app or account.

How this works

  1. Scan the QR code

    Point your phone’s camera at the code posted next to the printer and tap the link that appears.

  2. Choose your documents

    Add PDFs or photos, give the job a name you’ll recognize, and set the number of copies.

  3. Collect your pages

    Tap Print and your job goes straight to that printer. Once accepted, your document will print in a few moments.
